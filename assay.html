Vendor says 40% cheaper, 30% faster, half the tokens. Assay sets each claim against third-party benchmarks and your own test tasks, then strikes a hallmark: the gain is real, partly real, or marketing.

How a hallmark is struck

Assay divides the measured gain by the claimed gain. Deliver at least 80% of the promise and the claim is hallmarked; 40 to 80% is plated; under 40%, or a move in the wrong direction, is fool's gold. A gain that costs measured quality is capped at plated. Try it.

The release watch

Every Monday at 09:00 IST a GitHub Actions job lists new models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, reads their release notes, runs the test packs within a call budget and publishes a fresh audit report.
