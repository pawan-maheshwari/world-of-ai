      Signals

      Provenance record

      Frequency fingerprint

      Log spectrum of the noise residual from a 256 px crop at native resolution. Bright off-centre dots on the ringed points are periodic patterns that generator upsampling layers tend to leave. JPEG's 8×8 blocks can create similar dots, so this signal counts less for JPEGs.

      How it works

      A pipeline modelled on SynthID Detector

      SynthID Detector follows three steps: upload content, scan it for an embedded SynthID watermark, then show the result with the most likely watermarked portions highlighted. Origin Lens keeps that shape and the idea of highlighting portions, but replaces the watermark key, which only Google holds, with open signals that work on content from any generator.

      1

      Ingest

      Paste text or open a Word, PDF or Excel file; drop, paste or choose an image. Files are read in the page, never uploaded.

      SynthIDUpload content
      2

      Normalise

      Text: rejoin PDF lines, drop page numbers, set headings apart, split into sentences and words. Image: read metadata sections, convert to luminance, cut a 32 px tile grid, take native-resolution patches.

      SynthIDPrepare media for the detector
      3

      Extract signals in parallel

      Independent detectors each return a 0–1 AI-likelihood with an explanation.

      Text lane
      • Sentence-length rhythm
      • AI-typical phrase lexicon
      • Transition-word openers
      • Punctuation fingerprint (em dashes, "not only… but")
      • Word length and paragraph uniformity
      • Human markers (contractions, first person, casual tone)
      • Claude writing check: polish, cadence, generic framing; flags passages
      Image lane
      • Declared provenance: C2PA manifest, IPTC "trained algorithmic media", generator tags
      • Camera capture data in EXIF
      • Sensor-noise residual per tile
      • Spectral peaks from upsampling
      • Generator-native dimensions and camera-shaped frames
      • Claude vision check: anatomy, lettering, lighting, AI motifs
      SynthIDScan for the watermark
      4

      Fuse and calibrate

      Weighted logistic fusion maps signals to 0–100. A declared AI provenance tag sets the score to 95 (80 for AI composites); camera exposure data scales it down. Pixel statistics alone are capped at 50. When the Claude check runs it carries 70% of the score.

      SynthIDWatermark detected or not
      5

      Localise

      Each sentence and image tile gets its own score, blended with the overall one and with the passages Claude flags. Their share answers "how much of this is AI".

      SynthIDHighlight the portions most likely watermarked
      6

      Report

      Score band, share flagged, confidence, signal breakdown, provenance record and a copyable plain-text report.

      Claude check settings

      Use your own Anthropic API key

      On this site the Claude check runs on your own key. It is kept only in this browser and sent only to api.anthropic.com with each check you run. Each check costs a few cents of API credit at most.
