What this shows, and what it doesn't

  • The sample seasons are synthetic. They prove the machinery works, not that it works for your business.
  • The big gains are in decisions (which lines, how deep, what to hold back), not point forecasts. Carryover accuracy is a tie.
  • Every pre-season method under-forecast new lines by about 20% in season 2. Only the week-3 early read corrected it.
  • The early read assumed even weekly selling. Real launches spike or start slow, so real gains will be smaller.
  • That is why every pilot starts with a back-test on your own past seasons.
